HOW IT WORKS

Everything you need to know

📄
COLLECT PAGES

Each level has 20 pages floating above platforms. Some are gold — collect them for a quote from the blog post and bonus points.

🎯
BOSS PLATFORM

Collect 18 pages and a glowing boss platform appears at the top. Jump on it to complete the level. More satisfying than a simple counter.

❤️
POWER-UPS

Hearts give you one death save per level. Magnets pull nearby pages toward you for 10 seconds. Both spawn rarely — use them well.

🏆
ACHIEVEMENTS

Clear a level to unlock its achievement and character skin. Replay any level you've already cleared. All progress is saved locally.

SPEED RUN MODE

A completely
different challenge.

Toggle Speed Run mode before starting a level. Your timer starts on the first page you collect and stops when you land on the boss platform. Personal bests are saved per level.

  • Timer starts on first page collect — not on spawn
  • Quote overlays are disabled in speed run mode
  • Personal best shown above the canvas
  • Separate from normal play — progress is independent
