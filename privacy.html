Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

PixelWeirdo (pixelweirdo.com) is a blog about gaming, life lessons, and the science behind why we play. This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected when you visit, how it is used, and what your rights are.

1. Who we are

PixelWeirdo is operated by Pixelated Wisdom. For any privacy-related questions you can reach us at mao@pixelweirdo.com.

2. What data we collect

We do not collect personal information directly. However, the following third-party services may collect data when you visit this site:

  • Google AdSense — serves advertisements on this site and may use cookies to show relevant ads based on your browsing behaviour.
  • Google Analytics (if enabled) — collects anonymised data about how visitors use the site, such as pages visited and time spent.
  • Patreon — if you click through to our Patreon page, Patreon's own privacy policy applies.
  • Newsletter provider — if you subscribe to our newsletter, your email address is stored by our email provider and used only to send you new posts.

3. Cookies

This site uses cookies primarily through Google AdSense. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. They are used to:

  • Serve relevant advertisements
  • Measure ad performance
  • Understand how visitors use the site

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some features.

4. Google AdSense and advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

For more information on how Google uses data, see How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

5. Affiliate links

Some posts on PixelWeirdo contain affiliate links (for example, links to games on Amazon or other retailers). If you click one of these links and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We only link to products we genuinely recommend.

6. Third-party links

Our posts may link to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

7. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union, you have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data we hold about you. Since we collect minimal data directly, most requests will relate to the third-party services listed above. To exercise your rights, contact us at mao@pixelweirdo.com.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at mao@pixelweirdo.com.
